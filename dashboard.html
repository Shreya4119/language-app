Web push NOT BUILT
Needs a service worker, a VAPID key pair and a subscription store. No account required, so this ships first.
✕
Email BLOCKED
The first screen promises no account, no forms, nothing leaves the device. Email needs an address, a server, a privacy policy and a GDPR basis. Product decision, not a copy decision.
Templates for all three are written either way, so the channel work is plumbing and not authoring.

From the teacher, not the app
It contains the language
One a day, at an hour they chose
